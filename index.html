What you get

Two Workers

Not tools you babysit. Workers that do the busywork and hand it back — built into your own systems, your data stays yours.

Worker 1 · Proposal Drafter
Kills the proposal grind. Keeps the design 100% yours.
Intake
"Here's the project — the context, the client, the sector."
Processing
Drafts to ~80% from your own past work — your language, your projects — with a captioned image shortlist matched to the sector.
Handoff
A package organized 1:1 to your InDesign template's sections — each section's narrative + its images, in order. Not a competing design tool.
You finish
Place-and-polish in your template — 20–40 min vs 2–5 days. Every layout call stays yours.
Outcome: the 60–70% that's hunting + rewriting + image-swapping is gone. Your design bar — the part that wins the work — is untouched.
Gamma / AI-layout stays an optional side-lane for quick, low-stakes responses only — never the finish surface for a real SOQ.
Worker 2 · Lead Desk
Your list, cleaned and kept live. You own the BD — this handles the busywork behind it.
Intake
Drop in the orgs you're chasing, or upload a relevant list.
Processing
Researches the org AND the decision-maker — the verified contact and the angle — kept current.
Sign-off
You and Jada decide who's worth it. You keep lead-selection.
Into your stack
Lands clean in Factor, trackable, easy to split with Jada.
Outcome: the list stays clean, current, and researched — so your and Jada's time goes to the outreach, not the busywork.
What this is not. Not hourly. Not an MSP. We don't do your business development — you own the list, the relationships, and the outreach. We build and run the tools behind it, in your systems, and you own the data.

FAQ

The quick answers

Are you taking over my business development?
No. You own the list, the people, and the outreach. Lead Desk just does the research and keeps the list clean behind you.
Does this replace my proposal design?
No. It hands you an ~80% content package organized to your own InDesign template — you place and finish. Your design bar is what wins the work; we don't touch it.
Where does it live?
In your own systems — your stack, your data. It lands in Factor, import-ready. You own everything.
How fast is it running?
Setup gets the Proposal Drafter live on a real proposal first, so you see it produce before the full build. Then it runs.
